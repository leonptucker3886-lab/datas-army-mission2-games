# Data's Army Events Kit: Team Games & Niche Add-ons (Mission 2)

White-label modules for the corporate events app. Every module takes the reseller's logo, colors and name.

## Team games
1. **Office Trivia Showdown.** Hosts load questions (or pick packs: company facts, pop culture, holiday). Teams answer on phones, live leaderboard on the big screen. Settings: rounds, timer, team size.
2. **Two Truths & a Lie.** Each player submits three statements; others vote on the lie. Points for fooling people and for spotting lies. Great icebreaker for new hires.
3. **Photo Scavenger Hunt.** Host posts a list ("team selfie with the CEO", "best desk plant"). Teams upload photos; host or audience votes. Gallery exports after the event.
4. **Guess Who (Baby Photo Edition).** Staff upload childhood photos privately; the app shows them one at a time and players guess who. Opt-in only.
5. **Bingo Breaker.** Auto-generated bingo cards with people facts ("has run a marathon"). Players find colleagues who match and tap to confirm.

## Niche add-ons (sold per event or per month)
- **Holiday Pack:** Secret Santa hookup, ugly-sweater voting, holiday trivia packs.
- **Remote Team Pack:** video-friendly game timers, time-zone aware scheduling.
- **Awards Night:** nominations, anonymous voting, auto-made certificate PDFs.
- **Wellness Challenge:** step or habit challenges with team leaderboards.
- **Onboarding Pack:** new-hire icebreakers and a "meet the team" quiz.

## White-label settings (every module)
Logo, brand colors, custom domain, event name, welcome text, sponsor slot, and on/off per game.

Status: design spec. Not yet built into the app; build pairs with the white-label shell.

## How-to: rebrand and run an event
1. **Log in** to the partner dashboard and open **Brand Settings**.
2. **Upload your logo** and pick your brand colors. Add your custom domain if you have one.
3. Go to **Events > New Event**. Name it and set the date.
4. **Choose games** and add-ons. Turn each one on or off.
5. **Load content:** trivia questions, scavenger list, bingo facts. Or use the ready-made packs.
6. **Share the join link or QR code** with guests. They play from their phones; no app download.
7. **Run it live** from the host screen. Show the leaderboard on a TV or projector.
8. **After the event,** export photos, results and award certificates as PDFs.
