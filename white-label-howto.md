# Data's Army Events Kit: White-Label How-To (Mission 2)

## Rebrand and run an event
1. **Log in** to the partner dashboard and open **Brand Settings**.
2. **Upload your logo** and pick your brand colors. Add your custom domain if you have one.
3. Go to **Events > New Event**. Name it and set the date.
4. **Choose games** and add-ons. Turn each one on or off.
5. **Load content:** trivia questions, scavenger list, bingo facts. Or use the ready-made packs.
6. **Share the join link or QR code** with guests. They play from their phones; no app download.
7. **Run it live** from the host screen. Show the leaderboard on a TV or projector.
8. **After the event,** export photos, results and award certificates as PDFs.

Note: menu names are draft and will be matched to the built dashboard before release.
